# Student Support Hub

Create a modern, trustworthy, student-friendly mental health and counselling platform designed specifically for university and college students. The platform should allow students to access professional psychological counselling and trained peer-counselling services in a private, respectful and confidential environment.

Website Pages

Create the following pages:

Home

Clear headline such as: "A Safe Space to Talk, Be Heard and Get Support."

Brief explanation of the platform and who it is for.

Separate options for Professional Counselling and Peer Counselling.

Prominent buttons: "Book a Counselling Session", "Talk to a Peer Counsellor" and "Get Help Now".

Short explanation of privacy and confidentiality.

Mental health resources and educational content.

Testimonials presented anonymously, with consent.

Professional Counselling

Explain how students can access qualified counsellors or psychologists.

Counsellor profiles showing qualifications, areas of support and availability, without unnecessarily exposing sensitive information.

Appointment booking system.

Options for online and in-person sessions where available.

Session duration and applicable fees.

Peer Counselling

Explain what peer counselling is and how it differs from professional psychological services.

Profiles of trained peer counsellors using first names, initials or chosen display names where appropriate.

Allow students to choose a peer counsellor based on availability and areas of interest.

Private messaging or appointment requests.

Clearly explain that peer counsellors are trained supporters and are not substitutes for licensed mental-health professionals.

About Us

Mission and vision.

Explain the purpose of creating a safe and accessible support system for students.

Explain how counsellors and peer counsellors are trained, supervised and supported.

Explain the platform's commitment to privacy, confidentiality, respect and non-judgmental support.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Make this a highly visible page rather than hiding it in the footer.

Clearly explain what information is collected, why it is collected, who can access it and how it is protected.

Explain confidentiality in simple student-friendly language.

Clearly explain the limited circumstances where confidentiality may need to be broken, such as situations involving immediate safety concerns or where required by law.

Include a consent section before students submit sensitive information.

Do not expose counselling conversations, appointment details or personal information publicly.

FAQ
Include questions such as:

Is counselling confidential?

Can I talk to a peer counsellor anonymously?

Who can access my information?

What happens during my first counselling session?

Can I choose my counsellor?

Can I cancel or reschedule?

How much does counselling cost?

What happens if I need more specialised support?

Can I access support remotely?

What happens in an emergency?

Mental Health Resources

Stress and academic pressure.

Anxiety and emotional wellbeing.

Relationships and social challenges.

Bereavement and difficult life events.

Academic burnout.

Self-care and healthy coping strategies.

Information about when to seek professional help.

Links to appropriate emergency and professional support services.

Contact

Secure contact form.

General enquiries.

Counselling centre contact details.

Operating hours.

Physical location and Google Maps where applicable.

Emergency-support information.

Website Design

Use a calm, welcoming and professional design that feels safe to students rather than clinical or intimidating.

Use a strong but calming brand colour such as blue, teal or green, combined with white and neutral colours.

Use friendly photographs of diverse university students and counsellors, but avoid images that stereotype mental illness or make students feel judged.

Use clear icons for:

Professional counselling

Peer counselling

Confidentiality

Appointment booking

Mental health resources

Emergency support

Make the website fully responsive and easy to use on mobile phones.

Navigation

Top navigation:

Home | Professional Counselling | Peer Counselling | Resources | About | FAQ | Contact

Include prominent buttons:

Book a Session | Talk to a Peer

Also include a clearly visible Get Help Now option for urgent support.

Student Account and Booking System

Allow students to create secure accounts and:

Book appointments.

Select professional or peer counselling.

View available appointment times.

Receive appointment confirmations.

Reschedule or cancel appointments.

View upcoming appointments.

Receive secure notifications.

Manage their profile and privacy preferences.

Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

Privacy and Security

Build privacy into the website from the beginning.

Use:

HTTPS/SSL encryption.

Secure authentication.

Strong password requirements.

Two-factor authentication where appropriate.

Encrypted storage and transmission of sensitive information.

Role-based access so counsellors only access information they are authorised to see.

Secure session notes that cannot be accessed by ordinary users.

Audit logs for access to sensitive records.

Automatic logout/session timeout.

Clear consent and privacy notices.

Secure deletion and data-retention procedures.

Do not display students' real names, counselling history, appointments or conversations publicly.

Payment Integration

Design the platform so that counselling can be either free, subsidised or paid depending on the service.

For paid services, provide a secure payment system with options suitable for students in Kenya, such as:

M-Pesa / Safaricom

Card payments

Mobile money

Bank payment where appropriate

Use a reputable payment gateway rather than storing card details directly on the website.

After payment, automatically generate a secure payment confirmation and link it to the student's appointment without exposing payment information to peer counsellors.

Include:

Service price before payment.

Payment confirmation.

Transaction/reference number.

Refund/cancellation policy.

Payment history visible only to the student and authorised administrators.

Consider offering:

Free peer counselling.

Subsidised professional counselling.

Pay-per-session professional counselling.

University-sponsored counselling.

Counselling packages where appropriate.

A "request financial assistance" option for students who cannot afford a session.

Emergency and Safety Feature

Include a clearly visible Get Help Now button.

This should not attempt to replace emergency services. Instead, it should provide immediate information about appropriate emergency contacts, nearby professional services and trusted support channels relevant to the student's location.

Admin Dashboard

Create a secure administrator dashboard where authorised staff can:

Manage counsellors and peer counsellors.

Approve and manage profiles.

Manage appointments.

Monitor platform usage using anonymised statistics.

Manage payments and refunds.

Publish mental-health resources.

Manage FAQs.

Handle technical support requests.

Do not give administrators unnecessary access to private counselling conversations or clinical information.

Overall Goal

The final website should feel like a safe digital student wellbeing centre, not a social media platform. Prioritise trust, confidentiality, accessibility, simplicity and student dignity. The platform should make it easy for a student who is nervous about asking for help to take the first step without feeling judged or exposed.
